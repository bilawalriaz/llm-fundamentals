LoRA: Low-Rank Adaptation

Hu et al. · arXiv:2106.09685 · Jun 2021
ΔW = BA where r << d,k. 10,000x fewer params, zero inference latency. r=4 often enough. Adapt W_q + W_v.
07

QLORA

Dettmers et al. · arXiv:2305.14314 · May 2023
4-bit NF4 + double quantization + paged optimizers. 65B on one GPU. LoRA on ALL layers, not just attention.

SFT-DPO Interaction

FFT beats LoRA by more than SFT beats DPO. Parameterization > objective. Data scale is king.
Tier 3: Alignment

LIMA: Less Is More

Zhou et al. · arXiv:2305.11206 · May 2023
Superficial alignment hypothesis. 65B + 1,000 examples = 43% parity with GPT-4. Quality > quantity.
10

Direct Preference Optimization

Rafailov et al. · arXiv:2305.18290 · May 2023
RLHF as classification loss. r(x,y) = β·log(π/π_ref). Z(x) cancels. Dynamic gradient weighting.
11

ZEPHYR: Distilled Alignment

Tunstall et al. · arXiv:2310.16944 · Oct 2023
dSFT + AI Feedback + dDPO. 7B surpasses Llama2-Chat-70B. SFT required before DPO. Overfitting not harmful.
12

ORPO: Monolithic Alignment

Hong et al. · arXiv:2403.07691 · Mar 2024
Merge SFT + preference in one loss. Odds ratio > probability ratio. No reference model. 2 forward passes.
